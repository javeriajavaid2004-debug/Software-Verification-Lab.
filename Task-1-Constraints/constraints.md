# Task 1 - Identify Constraints

| Constraint ID | Constraint | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | The barrier must not open while a train is present in the crossing. | To prevent accidents. |
| C2 | The barrier must close when a train approaches the crossing. | To stop road traffic from entering the crossing. |
| C3 | Warning lights must turn ON when a train approaches. | To warn drivers and pedestrians about the approaching train. |
| C4 | The audible alarm must turn ON when a train approaches. | To alert nearby people about the approaching train. |
| C5 | The barrier must remain closed while the train is passing. | To keep road traffic away from the crossing. |
| C6 | The barrier must open only after the system confirms that the train has completely cleared the crossing. | To prevent accidents between road traffic and the train. |
| C7 | The system must detect sensor failure. | To identify problems that may affect safe operation. |
| C8 | The system must not treat the barrier as safely closed when a barrier failure is detected. | To avoid unsafe assumptions about the barrier position. |
| C9 | The system must detect communication loss. | To identify communication problems in the system. |
| C10 | The system must respond safely to emergency conditions. | To protect people, vehicles, and the train during emergencies. |
